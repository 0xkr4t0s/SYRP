# WP-2.1-11: syrp-dsp-filters: IIR and FIR filters, FilterDesign, SVF and TPT filters, Linkwitz-Riley, Ladder, Ballistics and DelayLine

## Purpose
Wave 2.1 item 11: `syrp-dsp-filters`, the filters of `juce_dsp`, implemented on WP-2.1-10's `DspProcessor` model and lane-generic over `syrp_simd::Sample` (ADR 0003 D5), so `Filter<F32x8>` runs eight filters in one pass. Master plan §4.9 says the filters are rebuilt from public-domain DSP literature (the RBJ Audio EQ Cookbook, Zavalishin's *The Art of VA Filter Design* for TPT and SVF structures, standard Butterworth, Chebyshev and elliptic design) to match JUCE's documented responses, measured with the `filter` profile (master plan §9.3: magnitude within ±0.01 dB and phase within ±0.1° from 20 Hz to 20 kHz at three sample rates, identical latency). `DelayLine` and `BallisticsFilter` are here because the effects (WP-2.1-12) and convolution (WP-2.1-13) build on them. This WP creates `syrp-dsp-filters`. Delivery: 4 PRs, each at most 1,200 changed lines (B1–B2, then B3, then B4–B5, then B6–B7).

## Behaviour
- B1. IIR: `IirCoefficients<T>` with the `make_*` designs the `dsp::IIR::Coefficients` documentation lists (first-order and second-order low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, all-pass, low shelf, high shelf and peak), `magnitude_for_frequency`, `phase_for_frequency` and their array forms, and `order()`; `ArrayCoefficients` for the same designs without allocation. `IirFilter<S>` implements `DspProcessor` (transposed direct form II, `snap_to_zero` as documented) and shares coefficients through an `Arc` swapped on the message thread, as JUCE's reference-counted coefficients are, never locked on the audio thread.
- B2. FIR: `FirCoefficients<T>` (taps, `magnitude_for_frequency`, `phase_for_frequency`, `normalise`) and `FirFilter<S>` (direct form with a circular history; latency reported as the documentation states).
- B3. `FilterDesign`: `design_fir_lowpass_window_method` (with each `WindowingFunction` kind), `design_fir_lowpass_kaiser_method`, `design_fir_lowpass_transition_method`, `design_fir_lowpass_least_squares_method`, `design_fir_lowpass_half_band_equiripple_method`, `design_iir_lowpass_high_order_butterworth_method` (and the Chebyshev I and II and elliptic methods), and `design_iir_lowpass_half_band_polyphase_allpass_method`, with the arguments the `dsp::FilterDesign` documentation gives. Tests check each design meets its stated passband ripple, stopband attenuation and transition band, and FIR designs are linear phase. Where the documentation does not fix a design detail that changes the coefficients, choose, pin it with a `b3_..._syrp_choice` test and list it under "Open questions" in the crate `AGENTS.md` for the harness.
- B4. State-variable and first-order: `StateVariableTptFilter<S>` (low-pass, band-pass and high-pass, `set_cutoff_frequency`, `set_resonance`, `process_sample(channel, x)`), the legacy `StateVariableFilter` with its `Parameters` (as documented), and `FirstOrderTptFilter<S>` (low-pass, high-pass, all-pass). Parameter changes are allowed every sample without blowing up (a proptest over random modulation keeps outputs finite and bounded).
- B5. `LinkwitzRileyFilter<S>` (low-pass, high-pass and all-pass outputs and `process_sample` returning both bands; the low and high bands sum to an all-pass, tested), `LadderFilter<S>` (the modes, cutoff, resonance and drive the `dsp::LadderFilter` documentation lists; `nonlinear` profile), and `BallisticsFilter<S>` (attack and release times, peak and RMS levels, as documented; `nonlinear` profile for envelope times).
- B6. `DelayLine<S, I>` with interpolation `None`, `Linear`, `Lagrange3rd` and `Thiran`: `new(max_delay)`, `set_delay(f32)` (clamped to the maximum), `push_sample(channel, x)`, `pop_sample(channel, delay, update_read_pointer)`, `set_maximum_delay_in_samples` (a message-thread call that reallocates), and the latency each interpolation adds, as documented. Tests: integer delays are exact, fractional delays match each interpolator's analytic response, and Thiran stays stable across its documented range.
- B7. Every `process` and per-sample call is allocation-free (`syrp_rt::no_alloc` tests), and every filter's lane-pack path equals its scalar path within `f32-arith` for random coefficients and inputs (ADR 0003 D7). Errors continue `errors/dsp.toml`.

## Not in this WP

## Rules that apply to every WP
- Clean room: work only from this spec, the master plan (`docs/design/master-plan.md`) and public JUCE *documentation* for behaviour. Never fetch or recall JUCE source code.
- Name every test after the behaviour it proves, prefixed with its id, for example `b3_try_push_fails_when_full`.
- Run `cargo syrp wp show WP-2.1-11` first and `cargo syrp wp check WP-2.1-11` last. A PR is ready only when that command exits 0.
- Shared files (workspace `Cargo.toml`, CI, `deny.toml`) are not yours. If you need a new third-party dependency in `[workspace.dependencies]`, declare it with an explicit version in your crate's own `Cargo.toml` and list it in the PR front matter `deps_added`; the adversarial verifier reviews it.
